Jollof Rice

Description

Jollof rice is a classic West African party dish. Rice is cooked in a rich sauce of tomatoes, red peppers and onions until every grain is soaked in flavour.

The best versions have a slightly smoky taste from the bottom of the pot. It is usually served with fried plantain, chicken or salad.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long grain parboiled rice
  • 6 fresh tomatoes
  • 3 red bell peppers (tatashe)
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers (rodo), to taste
  • 2 large onions
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt and seasoning cubes to taste

Steps

  1. Wash the rice until the water runs clear, then set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, red peppers, scotch bonnet and one onion until smooth.
  3. Boil the blended mix in a pot until most of the water has dried out.
  4. Heat the oil in a large pot and fry the second onion, sliced, until soft.
  5. Add the tomato paste and fry for 3 to 5 minutes, stirring often.
  6. Pour in the boiled pepper mix and fry until the oil rises to the top.
  7. Add the stock, curry, thyme, bay leaves, salt and seasoning cubes. Stir and taste.
  8. Add the rice and stir so it is fully coated in the sauce.
  9. Cover tightly and cook on low heat for 30 to 40 minutes, stirring once or twice.
  10. Check that the rice is soft. Add a little water if needed and cook a few more minutes.
  11. Let it rest for 5 minutes, then serve hot.
